# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy (POL-HR-021)

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Teams agree on at least one shared office day each week.

Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per year and requires HR and tax approval before travel.

Contoso reimburses up to USD 500 once every two years for home-office equipment such as a monitor or chair.
