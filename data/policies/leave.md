# Parental and Family Leave Policy (POL-HR-017)

Contoso provides 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave to the primary caregiver and 8 weeks to the secondary caregiver after the birth, adoption or foster placement of a child. Leave must start within 12 months of the event.

Employees become eligible after 90 days of continuous service. Part-time employees receive leave pro-rated to their contracted hours.

Up to 10 days of paid family-care leave per calendar year can be used to care for a sick child, partner or parent. Family-care leave does not roll over.

To request leave, submit the request in the HR portal at least 30 days before the planned start date, except in emergencies.
