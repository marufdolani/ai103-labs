# Compensation Review and Bonus Policy (POL-HR-030) - RESTRICTED

Annual bonus pools are set at 12% of eligible base salary for the company, adjusted by a company performance multiplier between 0.5 and 1.5.

Individual bonus recommendations are finalized by the compensation committee in February. This document is restricted to HR and executives.
