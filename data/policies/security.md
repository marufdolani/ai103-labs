# Information Security and Acceptable Use (POL-SEC-002)

All laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.

Customer data classified as Confidential must never be pasted into public AI tools. Approved internal AI assistants run on Microsoft Foundry with keyless authentication and data residency controls.

Report suspected phishing to security@contoso.example within 1 hour. Lost devices must be reported within 24 hours.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every account. Passwords must be at least 14 characters.
