# Travel and Expense Policy (POL-FIN-004)

Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Business class may be booked for flights of 6 hours or more with director approval.

Hotel spend is capped at USD 250 per night in tier-1 cities (New York, London, Tokyo, Sydney) and USD 180 elsewhere.

Daily meal allowance is USD 75 per day while travelling. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days with itemized receipts for any item above USD 25. Claims over USD 5,000 require CFO approval.
