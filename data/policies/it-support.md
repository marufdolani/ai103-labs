# IT Support and Equipment Policy (POL-IT-009)

Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Engineers may request a refresh after 2 years if build times exceed 10 minutes.

The IT service desk is available 24x7 at extension 4357 (HELP). Priority 1 incidents receive a response within 15 minutes.

Software not on the approved catalogue requires a security review, which takes up to 5 business days.
